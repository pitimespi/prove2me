# Read-back: `BitcoinSelfishMining.denominator_pos`

Let $\alpha$ be a real number with $0<\alpha$ and $\alpha<\tfrac12$ (both hypotheses named and usable). Then
$$
0 \;<\; 2\alpha^3 - 4\alpha^2 + 1 .
$$
The inequality is strict. The polynomial is written out literally in the statement; it coincides term by term with the custom definition `denom` $\alpha = 2\alpha^3-4\alpha^2+1$ from the preamble, but the statement does not mention `denom` by name. Nothing else (no $\gamma$, no states, no sums) is involved; the claim is a single real-polynomial inequality on the open interval $\alpha\in(0,\tfrac12)$, and says nothing about $\alpha$ outside that interval.
