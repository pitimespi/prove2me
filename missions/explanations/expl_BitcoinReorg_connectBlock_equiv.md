`CheckTxInputs` (`inputsLoop`, `haveCoin`), `SequenceLocks`, the sigop cost, `CheckInputScripts`, the undo record and the BIP30 scan read the ledger only through `getCoin`/`accessCoin`, so they are equal on equivalent ledgers; `connectLoop_equiv` is an induction over the transactions in which the step decisions are literally the same expression on both sides and `updateCoins_equiv` keeps the ledgers equivalent. `connectBlock` adds the genesis shortcut, the BIP30 test and the coinbase bound, none of which distinguishes the two.
