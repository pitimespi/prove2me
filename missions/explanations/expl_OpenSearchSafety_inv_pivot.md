First derive that descent is antisymmetric. Apply `inv_deepest_committed` to the base. If a deepest committed ancestor-or-self `D` exists, it is published (`inv_committed_published`) and is the witness; it remains to show that `x` or `y` is `D`'s last accepted configuration. If `D` is the base, the bootstrap case of `Realizes` is impossible (`D` has term at least 1), so the base is realized by a publication that `pub_unique` identifies with `D`'s, and `y` is its last accepted configuration. Otherwise the base is not committed (maximality and antisymmetry), so the realization is by the base's own configurations, `x` its last committed one, no committed key lies strictly between `D` and the base, and `inv_lineage` from `D` to the base gives `x` or `y` equal to `D`'s last accepted configuration. If no committed ancestor-or-self exists, the bootstrap case of `Realizes` gives `P.initConfs x` directly, and otherwise the base is uncommitted, `x` is its publication's last committed configuration, and `inv_root_lcc` applies.
