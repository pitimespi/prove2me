Each Cesàro average is a distribution with the uniform geometric tail bound, so the family is tight; along any subsequence a further subsequence converges pointwise (diagonal argument) to a distribution ν; the difference `dist (cesaro n) − cesaro n` is $O(1/n)$, so ν is invariant; by the uniqueness mission's `stationary_unique` ν is `stationary α`; a sequence all of whose subsequences have subsequences converging to the same limit converges.
