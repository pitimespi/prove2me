First a count. The number of pairs is a double sum over the first draw `x` and the second draw `y` (`Finset.card_filter`, `Fintype.sum_prod_type`); for fixed `x` the inner sum counts the `y` with `|t − randomDelay Mb y| < δ` for the fixed target `t = a + randomDelay Ma x − b`. The private lemma `card_close_le` bounds this by `(2δ − 1) · 2⌈2^63/Mmin⌉`: the delays in question lie in the open interval `(t − δ, t + δ)`, which contains `2δ − 1` integers (`Int.card_Ioo`), and each integer value has at most `2⌈2^63/Mb⌉ ≤ 2⌈2^63/Mmin⌉` preimages, by `randomDelay_near_uniform` and the private `ceil_div_anti` for values in `[1, Mb]`, and none otherwise by `randomDelay_range`; `Finset.card_le_mul_card_image_of_maps_to` combines the two. Summing over the `2^64` first draws bounds the count by `2^64 · (2δ − 1) · 2⌈2^63/Mmin⌉`. The inequality between rationals follows after casting the count, cross-multiplying (`div_le_div_iff₀`) and `ring`.
