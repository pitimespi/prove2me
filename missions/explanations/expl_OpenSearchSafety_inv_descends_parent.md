Case analysis on the descent. The reflexive case is excluded by the hypothesis that the keys differ. In the step case the descent reaches some key `(t', v')` and ends with a parent link from `(t', v')` to the publication's key, witnessed by a sent publication with that key; `pub_unique` identifies this publication with the given one, so its ghost parent `(t', v')` is `(pt, pv)`, and the remaining descent is the goal.
