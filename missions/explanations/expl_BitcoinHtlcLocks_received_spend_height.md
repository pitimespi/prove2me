`received_p2wsh_spend` turns the accepted witness into `ReceivedSpend` on `[pk, sig]`; the four-element disjunct is impossible for a two-element stack, the revocation disjunct is `RevocationSpend`, and the timeout disjunct gives `CltvPasses` with the concrete oracle, hence `checkLockTime` (`cltv_concrete`), hence `nLockTime < threshold`, `expiry ≤ nLockTime` and a non-final input (`checkLockTime_iff`); `isFinalTx_height` then gives `nLockTime < height`.
