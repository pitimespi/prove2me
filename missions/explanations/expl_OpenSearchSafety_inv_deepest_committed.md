Case on whether `(t, v)` is published. If it is not, a descent into it can only be reflexive (a parent link needs a publication of the key), and committed keys are published (`inv_committed_published`), so the right disjunct holds. If it is, take the descent from a term-0 key given by `pub_rooted` and induct on it. At the term-0 root the right disjunct holds: a committed key has term at least 1, while a key descending to a term-0 key has term 0 (`inv_descends_key`). In the step case every ancestor-or-self of the key is the key itself or an ancestor-or-self of its parent (`inv_descends_parent`). If the key is committed it is its own deepest committed ancestor; otherwise the induction hypothesis for the parent transfers, the parent's deepest committed ancestor serving for the key (`descends_trans`), or the absence of committed ancestors carrying over.
