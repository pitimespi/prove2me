A loop invariant for `sweep` (the private `sweep_spec`): the accumulated (fee, size) of each side are breakpoints of the corresponding diagram, and the two flags record exactly whether each diagram has been strictly above the other on the part swept so far; each iteration compares the next breakpoint of one diagram with the other diagram's segment at that size (a `ByRatio` comparison through `byRatio_eq_compare_ratio`), which is where a piecewise-linear comparison can change. At the end the flags decide the four outcomes, with the constant tails handled by `diagram_total`.
