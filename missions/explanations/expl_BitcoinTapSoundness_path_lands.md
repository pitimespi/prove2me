Induction on the path, through a private recursive helper. The empty path lands on the tree itself at depth 0. For a path `n :: rest`, the fold from `h` equals the fold from `tapbranchHash h n` over `rest`, which by the induction hypothesis lands on a subtree `U` at depth `rest.length`. `U` cannot be a leaf (`leaf_ne_branch`); if it is a branch, `branch_eq` (children's hashes are 32 bytes by `hash_length`) shows `h` is one of its children's hashes, and the children of a subtree at depth `d` are subtrees at depth `d + 1`.
