A counting argument. The key step shows that an eligible quorum `q` of a configuration `Z` built by `BootstrapConfig` names the resolved nodes of more than half of the `k` requirements, all matched. The map from requirement indices to members is injective (`resolve` injective, placeholders distinct from each other and from resolved ids), so `Z` has at least `k` members (`List.Nodup.length_le_of_subset`); every member of `Z` occurring in `q` is eligible, hence not a placeholder, hence the resolved node of a matched requirement it names; so the members of `Z` in `q` number at most the matched requirements whose node is in `q`, and the quorum condition gives more than `k / 2` of them (`omega`). Applied to `X, q₁` and to `Y, q₂`, the counting lemma `filter_length_add_le` over `List.range k` gives a requirement `i` counted in both, and `resolve i` is in `q₁` and in `q₂`.
