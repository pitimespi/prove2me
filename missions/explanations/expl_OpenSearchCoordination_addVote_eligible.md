Unfold `addVote` and split on its condition. If the node is eligible and its id absent, the new list is `n.id :: v.nodes`, membership in it is `x = n.id ∨ x ∈ v.nodes`, the eligibility conjunct is true, and the disjunction is commuted. Otherwise the collection is unchanged, and the failed condition says that an eligible node's id is already present, so the second disjunct of the right-hand side implies the first.
