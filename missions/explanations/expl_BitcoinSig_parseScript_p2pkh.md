`pushBytes h` for a 20-byte `h` is `0x14 :: h` (first branch of `pushBytes`). The script bytes `76 a9 14 h 88 ac` have length 25, so `parseScript` runs `parseScriptAux 25`; `simp` with `parseScriptAux` and `getOp` unfolds the five instructions — `0x76` and `0xa9` are non-push opcodes, `0x14` is a direct push of 20 bytes whose `take`/`drop` are `h` and `[0x88, 0xac]` (`List.take_left'`, `List.drop_left'`), then `0x88` and `0xac` — and the result matches `p2pkhScript h` once the four opcode constants are evaluated (`decide`).
