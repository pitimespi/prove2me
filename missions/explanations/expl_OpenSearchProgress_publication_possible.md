The theorem is the private lemma `pub_possible_core`, which builds the execution. The acceptance phase (`pub_accept_phase`, an induction over `Q`) makes each node of `Q` still below `t` handle a start-join from the cluster-manager for `t` (`startJoin_ok`; the stable period allows a request of term `t` at a node of `Q`); then, by `pub_q_ready` applied with `pub_c` at the current cluster, either the node's acknowledgement of `(t, s.version)` has already been sent, or it accepts `s` and sends it; the nodes of `Q` stay in terms at most `t`. The counting phase (`pub_count_phase`, an induction over `Q`) makes the cluster-manager handle each acknowledgement, which succeeds (`respond_ok`, with `pub_c`) and counts the eligible sender (`addVote_mem`), earlier votes being kept. Finally the cluster-manager handles the acknowledgement of one member of `Q` once more (messages are never removed); its votes then contain `Q`, which by `pub_quorum` and `pub_c` is a publish quorum, so the handler returns the commit of `(t, s.version)` (`respond_ok`), which is sent.
