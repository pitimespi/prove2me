Induction on `Reachable`. The genesis ledger is empty. For a step, `acceptance` turns the accepted outcome into `Valid`, and `apply_spec` makes the new ledger `applyTxs` of the old one (or the old one, on the genesis-hash shortcut). A coin of `applyTxs` is a coin of the old ledger or an output of one of the block's transactions (`mem_applyTxs`, through `mem_updateCoins`, `mem_addCoinsFrom`, `mem_spendInputs`); the former is in range by the induction hypothesis, the latter by `TxSpec` from `BlockSpec`.
