The committed key is published (`inv_committed_published`), so `pub_rooted` gives a descent to it from a term-0 key. Strengthen the goal to: for every key on that chain with some committed ancestor-or-self, a first committed key exists; then induct on the descent. The term-0 root has no committed ancestor (committed keys have term at least 1, `inv_descends_key`). In the step case, if some committed key is an ancestor-or-self of the parent, the induction hypothesis gives the answer, extended to the key by the last link. Otherwise the given committed ancestor-or-self must be the key itself, since every ancestor-or-self of the key is the key or an ancestor-or-self of its parent (`inv_descends_parent`); and it is first because any committed ancestor-or-self of it is, by the same split, the key itself.
