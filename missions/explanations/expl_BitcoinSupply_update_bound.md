`updateCoins` is `addCoinsFrom` over the outputs after (for a non-coinbase) `spendInputs`. On a distinct-key ledger `spendCoin` removes exactly the coin at the key (`totalValue_spendCoin`), so with distinct, present prevouts `spendInputs` lowers the total by exactly `valueIn` (`totalValue_spendInputs`, using that spending one input does not change the other inputs' coins). `addCoin` raises the total by at most the new value when stored coins are non-negative (an overwritten coin can only lower it, an unspendable output is not stored), so `addCoinsFrom` adds at most `sumValues` of the outputs.
