The theorem is the private lemma `won_core`. The safety invariant holds at `W'` (`reachable_inv`; every stable step is a step of the model, `estep_step`). By `elect_c` the candidate's last accepted configuration at `W'` is the one it had at `W`, and its last committed configuration is either its initial last committed configuration or its initial last accepted one (a commit copies the latter into the former). Case on `electionWon`: if it were false, the invariant's field `not_won_no_quorum` says the join votes are not a quorum of both configurations; but they contain `Q` (`hvotes`), a quorum of each possible configuration (`hquorum`), so `quorum_mono` gives both quorums, a contradiction. The hypotheses `hQ` and `hct` are not used by the proof.
