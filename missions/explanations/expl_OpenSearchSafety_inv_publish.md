The private lemma `spec_publish` gives that the state's term is the node's current term, that the state is newer than the accepted one or of term 0, the response, and the new state (only the accepted state changes). Since the publication has term at least 1 (`pub_wf`), the Zen1 branch is excluded and an eligible node's accepted key strictly increases (`hinc`). An elected node accepts only its own publications (`won_unique`), and in fact exactly its publication in flight (`hlead`: `leader_settled` is excluded by freshness, `leader_inflight` gives the publication in flight, and `inv_descends_parent` with `inv_descends_key` show that the accepted publication's version is the last published version, so `pub_unique` identifies them). The fields: `won_quorum` from `leader_pubs`; `not_won_no_quorum` because a node that has not won and holds a quorum of the new accepted configuration would share a voter with the publisher's voters (`pub_voters`, `quorum_intersect`), so `join_unique` makes it the publisher, contradicting `pub_source_term` and `votes_started`; `joinVotes_sent` because `Below` survives the increase of the accepted key; `accepted_realizes` by the publication itself; `join_after_response` and `response_le` for the new response because the node's joins have terms at most its current term; the leader facts through `hlead`; everything else by monotonicity.
