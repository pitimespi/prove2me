Let `S j` be the number of draws `X` whose rounds before `j` are all contested; `S 0 = (2^64)^(nK)`. For `j < K`, `S (j + 1) ≤ contestedBound n δ Mmin · S j`: both sums are split into a sum over the draws `r` of the other rounds and a sum over the draws `y` of round `j` (`sum_piSplitAt`). By the causality hypothesis `hcausal`, for fixed `r` neither the event "the rounds before `j` are contested" nor the offsets and bounds of round `j` depend on `y`: they are those of the draw `X0` with round `j` set to zero. The inner sum for `S (j + 1)` is therefore the indicator of that event at `X0` times the number of contested draws of round `j` for the fixed offsets and bounds, which `contested_prob_le` bounds by `contestedBound n δ Mmin · (2^64)^n`, while the inner sum for `S j` is the same indicator times `(2^64)^n`. Induction on `j` gives `S K ≤ contestedBound n δ Mmin ^ K · (2^64)^(nK)`, and `S K` is the count in the statement (every `k : Fin K` is below `K`).
