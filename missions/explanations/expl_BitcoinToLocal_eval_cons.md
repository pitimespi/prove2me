Symbolic execution of `run` over the nine instructions, with per-opcode `step` lemmas (executing, skipped, and conditional) and `execOp` equations for `OP_IF`, `OP_ELSE`, `OP_ENDIF`, `OP_DROP`, `OP_CHECKSIG`; the small-integer instruction is handled for both encodings `CScript` uses (a 17-way case split for `OP_1NEGATE`/`OP_n`, the push otherwise). Stack-size checks are discharged from `tl.length + 1 ≤ 1000`; the final `OP_CHECKSIG` needs two elements, which forces `tl` nonempty.
