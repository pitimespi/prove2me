First derive that descent is antisymmetric (`inv_descends_key`). Strengthen the goal to: for every key `c'` reached from `d`, if some committed key strictly after `d` is an ancestor-or-self of `c'`, a closest one exists; then induct on the descent from `d`. In the reflexive case a committed key strictly after `d` and at or before `d` contradicts antisymmetry. In the step case split the ancestors-or-self of the current key into the key itself and those of its parent (`inv_descends_parent`). If some committed key strictly after `d` lies at or before the parent, use the induction hypothesis and extend by the last link; otherwise the committed key is the current key, and it is closest, because any committed key between `d` and it is either it or lies at or before the parent, hence is `d`.
