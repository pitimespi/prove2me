`toNum true 4 v` returns `none` when `v.length > 4`, `none` when minimality is required and `nonMinimal v` holds, and `some (setVch v)` otherwise. Under the hypothesis $|\mathrm{serialize}(n)| \le 4$ the first test fails; by `BitcoinScript.serialize_minimal` the second test fails; and by `BitcoinScript.serialize_roundtrip` the returned value is $n$. The proof is `unfold toNum`, two rewrites, and `simp`.
