We prove that the outdated-version test holds exactly when one of the three threshold conditions does. The test is a Boolean disjunction of three conjunctions, each a version comparison and an `activeAfter` test; `activeAfter` is the decidable inequality $\text{activation height} \le \text{prevHeight} + 1$. Reading the Booleans back as propositions and re-associating the disjunction gives the statement.
