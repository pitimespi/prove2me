Unfold `verifyTaprootCommitment` and rewrite its internal key and parity to the honest ones; `checkTapTweak` lifts the internal key to a point `P` (it fails otherwise, contradicting `HonestOutput`). If the control block's Merkle root is `T.hash`, `script_path_soundness` applies. Otherwise compare the two tweak hashes `tapTweakHash internal32 root` and `tapTweakHash internal32 T.hash`: if equal, `tagged_collision` gives either a collision or equal messages, and equal messages with the same 32-byte prefix give equal roots, a contradiction; if different, both pass `xonlyTweakAddCheck q parity P`, which is a `TweakCollision`.
