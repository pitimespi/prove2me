`acceptHeader_clock'`: for `now ≤ now'`, either the clock test fires at `now` (then the outcome at `now` is `futureTime` or an earlier failure that is identical at `now'`), or it does not fire at either clock and `acceptHeader` is literally the same term (`acceptHeader_clock`). With `validate S b now ≠ futureTime` the first case reduces to the second, and `validate` only reads the clock through `acceptHeader`.
