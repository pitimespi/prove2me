Induction on the transactions (`connectLoop_iff'`), generalizing the accumulator. Coinbase step: only the sigop test. Non-coinbase step: `checkTxInputs_iff` gives the fee `feeOf u tx` and the inputs predicate (the fee is non-negative because outputs are non-negative), the fee-range test becomes `fees + fee ≤ MAX_MONEY` since both are non-negative, `sequenceLocks_iff` and `inputScriptsOk_iff` convert the two Boolean tests, and the recursive call applies to `updateCoins u tx H` with the larger fee total.
