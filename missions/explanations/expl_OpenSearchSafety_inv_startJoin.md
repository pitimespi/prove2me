The private lemma `spec_startJoin` gives that the request's term `t` exceeds the current term, the node's new state (term `t`, transient state reset, accepted state unchanged) and the join, from `P.dn n` (`localNode_eq`) to `P.dn src` in term `t`, with the node's last accepted term and version-or-metadata-version. Helper facts characterize the new set of messages (one new join) and show that every publish response has term at least 1 and that the node's earlier joins have terms below `t` (`join_term_le`). Winning only shrinks: the node is no longer elected. The fields: the node's transient facts hold trivially after the reset; `term_le` because the accepted term was at most the old term; `join_wf` because the requester is eligible; `join_term_le` and `join_unique` because the old joins of the node have smaller terms; `join_after_response` for the new join because each publish response of the node has term at least 1 and a key at most the node's accepted key (`response_le`), so the accepted term is at least 1 and the join carries the version itself (`vomv_of_pos`); `pub_source_term` because the node's publications have terms at most its old term, now below `t`. Everything else carries over by the monotonicity lemmas.
