`acceptHeader_cases` lists the four possible header outcomes; only `accept S []` lets `validate` reach `connectBlock`. In that branch the accept constructor is injective, giving `S'.headers = S.headers ++ [b.header]` and `S'.utxo = u` with `connectBlock S b = some (u, undo)`, and `connectBlock_apply` gives `u = (applySpec S b).utxo`; the headers of `applySpec` are `S.headers ++ [b.header]` in both of its branches.
