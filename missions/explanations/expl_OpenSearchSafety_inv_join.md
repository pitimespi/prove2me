The private lemma `spec_join` gives the five passed guards and the new state: the vote of the join's source added, `electionWon` recomputed, and `lastPublishedVersion` set to the accepted version on a new win. With the default strategy, winning is having a quorum of both configurations (`hwq`). The new votes are the old ones and possibly the new voter, who is eligible (`hmem`); each is backed by a sent join `Below` the node's accepted key (`hvotes_sent`: the freshness guard becomes `Below` through `vomv_of_pos` when the accepted term is not 0); and a won election stays won (`quorum_mono`). For a new win the proof establishes: the accepted term is below the current term (`hnot_t`: otherwise the accepted state is a publication of the current term, whose publisher's voters (`pub_voters`) share a voter with the new votes (`quorum_intersect`), so `join_unique` makes the publisher the node itself, contradicting `pub_source_term`); the new votes form an election record of the node over its accepted key (`hrec_n`); no publication of the current term exists (`hno_pub`: the record of the term's first publication, from `inv_reign_first` and `pub_election`, and the node's record have the same candidate by `inv_election_unique`, again contradicting `pub_source_term`); and no other node has won the term (`hno_other`: an elected node of the same term either has a publication of the term, excluded, or by `leader_settled` an election record of its own, and `inv_election_unique` applies). These give `won_unique`. For the new winner, `leader_pubs` is vacuous (no publication of the term), `leader_inflight` does not apply (its last published version is now its accepted version), and `leader_settled` holds by its second disjunct. The other fields carry over, using `quorum_mono` for the grown votes.
