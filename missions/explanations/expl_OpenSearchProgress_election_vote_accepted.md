The theorem is the private lemma `vote_core`. Every stable step is a step of the safety model (`estep_step`), so the clusters of the stable execution are reachable and satisfy the safety invariant (`reachable_inv`). Two inductions over the stable execution do the work. `elect_c`: the candidate's accepted term, version, metadata version and last accepted configuration stay those it had at `W`, and once it has left its initial term it has handled a start-join since its last restart; it neither bootstraps nor restarts, it accepts no publication (one at most as fresh as its own accepted state fails `handlePublishRequest`'s freshness guard, the Zen1 branch being dead because every publication has term at least 1, `pub_wf`), and a commit only copies the accepted configuration into the committed one (`markCommitted_spec`). `elect_q`: every voter's accepted term and version-or-metadata-version stay no fresher than the candidate's initial ones, and so does every join for `t` from a voter to the candidate; no such join exists at `W` (`join_term_le`: a voter's joins have terms at most its current term, below `t`), a voter accepts only publications no fresher than `(kt, kv)` (of term at least 1, so their version is their version-or-metadata-version, `vomv_pos`), commits keep the compared values (`markCommitted_vomv`), and a new join carries the voter's current values. With `hct`, `hconf` and these facts the five guards of `handleJoin` pass (`join_ok`), and `spec_join` with `addVote_mem` shows the source counted: it is eligible, since a join comes from the `DiscoveryNode` of its source (`join_wf`) and `hQ`.
