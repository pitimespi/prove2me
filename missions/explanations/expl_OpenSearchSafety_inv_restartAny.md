The restarted node is not eligible, so every fact about persisted state, which the invariant states for eligible nodes only, concerns other nodes and is unchanged; so are the facts about the sources of joins, responses and publications, which are eligible. The node's transient state is reset by the constructor, which makes its transient facts trivial, and `won_unique` carries over because a node that wins is eligible, hence not the restarted node. The messages are unchanged, and the facts that mention them carry over.
