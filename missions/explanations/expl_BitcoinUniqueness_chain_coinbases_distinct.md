Induction over the chain generalizing the state: the tail is connected to the state after the first block (reachable by `Reachable.step`, next height one more by `apply_spec`), so its coinbases are pairwise distinct by the induction hypothesis; every block of the tail is accepted on a reachable state of next height at least one more than the first block's (an inner induction, `hlater`), so `coinbases_distinct` separates the first block's coinbase from each of them.
