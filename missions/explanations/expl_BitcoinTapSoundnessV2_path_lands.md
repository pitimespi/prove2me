Induction on the path through a private recursive helper, following `walk`. The empty path lands on $T$ itself. For `n :: rest`, the fold from $h$ equals the fold from `tapbranchHash h n` over `rest`, which by the induction hypothesis lands on a subtree $U$ or already returned a pair. If $U$ is a leaf, `leaf_ne_branch` gives the pair `walk` returns. If $U$ is a branch and $h$ equals a child's hash, `walk` descends into that child, a subtree one level deeper (`branch_children_mem`); otherwise `branch_eq` (children's hashes are 32 bytes by `hash_length`) excludes the equal-children case and gives the collision of the two `TapBranch` preimages, which is the pair `walk` returns.
