Induction on `RoundReachable`. A round that has just started has no entries. For a handled response, the private lemma `pv_handle_shape` (unfold `handlePreVoteResponse` and split its branches) says that the accepted state is unchanged and that the entries are either unchanged or the new pair, whose response passed the freshness test, followed by the old entries without its sender. In the second case the new pair is no fresher, and delivered by the step's hypothesis; the other pairs come from the old list (`List.mem_filter`) and the induction hypothesis; and the senders stay distinct because the new sender was filtered out of the rest, a sublist of the old list (`List.Nodup.sublist`). Closing changes no entry.
