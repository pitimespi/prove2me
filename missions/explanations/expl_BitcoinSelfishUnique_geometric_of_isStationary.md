Let $r = \alpha/(1-\alpha)$ and $q_n = \pi_{n+1} - r\pi_n$ on the leads. The balance equations `balance_two_of`/`balance_lead_of` give $(1-\alpha)\pi_{k+2} = \pi_{k+1} - \alpha\pi_k$, hence $q_{n+1} = q_n$ and $q_n = q_0 =: c$. Then $\pi_n - L = r^n(\pi_0 - L)$ with $L = c/(1-r)$, so $\pi_n \to L$ (`tendsto_pow_atTop_nhds_zero_of_lt_one`); the terms of the summable series tend to 0 (`Summable.tendsto_atTop_zero`), so $L = 0$ by uniqueness of limits, $c = 0$, and $\pi_{n+1} = r\pi_n$.
