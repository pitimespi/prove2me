Unfold `tapbranchHash` and case on `lexLt a b`. If it holds, `lexLt_asymm` gives `lexLt b a = false`, so both sides hash `a ++ b`. If it fails and `lexLt b a` holds, both sides hash `b ++ a`. If both fail, `lexLt_total` (induction on the two lists, with `BitVec.eq_of_toNat_eq` on equal heads) gives `a = b`. The two private lemmas are proved by structural recursion on the pair of lists, splitting on the two strict byte comparisons.
