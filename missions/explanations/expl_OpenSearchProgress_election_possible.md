The theorem is the private lemma `possible_core`, which builds the execution. The candidate's last accepted configuration is nonempty because it has a quorum (`hasQuorum_of_isEmpty`). First the candidate handles its own start-join request for `t` (`startJoin_ok`, its term being below `t`; the stable period allows it as the candidate's own request), which sends its own join. The start-join phase (`elect_sj_phase`, an induction over the list of voters) lets each voter still below `t` handle the candidate's request, which succeeds by `startJoin_ok`, keeping the invariant that the candidate and every voter are below `t` or have sent the candidate a join for `t` (the join is from the voter's local node, `localNode_id`); at the end every voter has sent one. The join phase (`elect_join_phase`, an induction over the voters) lets the candidate handle each voter's join, which is accepted and counted by `vote_core`, earlier votes being kept (`addVote_mem`). Finally `won_core` shows that the candidate has won.
