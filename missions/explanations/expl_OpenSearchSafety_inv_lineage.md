Generalize the target to every publication whose key is reached from `D` without committed keys strictly in between, and induct on the descent from `D`'s key. The reflexive case contradicts the distinctness of the keys. In the step case `pub_unique` identifies `E` with the publication of the last link, so `E`'s ghost parent is the previous key, of term at least 1 (`inv_descends_key`), and `pub_parent` gives the parent publication with the two reconfiguration rules: (R1) `E`'s last committed configuration is the parent's, or the parent's last accepted one if the parent is committed; (R2) if `E`'s last accepted configuration differs from the parent's, `E`'s last committed configuration is the parent's last accepted one. If the parent is `D` (by `pub_unique`), R2 gives the conclusion directly. Otherwise the parent lies strictly between `D` and `E`, so it is not committed (the hypothesis on committed keys, plus the strict version increase), R1 copies the parent's last committed configuration to `E`, and the induction hypothesis for the parent, combined with R2, gives the conclusion.
