A counting argument: each uniquely successful round $i$ strictly inside $(r_0, r)$ pins the honest block of round $i$ at a height (`height_unique`), and $D$, adversarial above its fork at round $r_0$, must fill each such height below $|D|$ with a distinct adversarial block of a round in $[r_0, r]$ (`run_le_sum_Z`, an injection into `advBlocks`). With `Typical` on the window $[r_0, r]$ and chain growth for `chain p r`, this bounds $|D|$ strictly below $|\text{chain } p\, r|$.
