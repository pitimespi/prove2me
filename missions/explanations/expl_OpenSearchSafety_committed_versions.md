Obtain the invariant from `reachable_inv`. For equal versions, `inv_committed_comparable` relates the two committed keys by descent, and `inv_descends_key` says that a descent either joins equal keys or strictly increases the version; equal versions exclude the second case, so the terms are equal. For `t₁ < t₂`, the second committed key is the key of a publication (`inv_committed_published`), `inv_leader_completeness` gives a descent from the first committed key to it, and `inv_descends_key` gives `v₁ < v₂`, the case of equal keys being excluded by the terms.
