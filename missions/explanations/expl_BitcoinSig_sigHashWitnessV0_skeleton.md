`sigHashWitnessV0` hashes `version`, `hashPrevouts` (a `flatMap` of `serOutPoint ∘ prevout` over `vin`), `hashSequence` (a `flatMap` of `ser32 ∘ nSequence`), the signed input's prevout and sequence, the script code, the amount, `hashOutputs` (over `vout`), lock time and hash type. The two `flatMap`s are obtained from the map equality by `List.flatMap_map` (`e1`, `e2`), the signed input's fields by `skel_getD`, and the rest by the hypotheses; `simp only` with these facts closes the goal.
