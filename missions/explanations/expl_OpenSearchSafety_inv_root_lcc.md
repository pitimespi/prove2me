Induct on the descent from a term-0 key given by `pub_rooted`, generalized to every publication of the key reached. The root case contradicts `pub_wf` (publications have term at least 1). In the step case `pub_unique` identifies the publication's ghost parent as the previous key. If the parent has term 0, `pub_wf` states that the last committed configuration is a bootstrap configuration. Otherwise `pub_parent` gives the parent publication; the parent is a proper ancestor and the publication has no committed proper ancestor, so the parent is not committed and R1 makes the last committed configuration the parent's; the induction hypothesis for the parent applies, because a committed ancestor-or-self of the parent would be a committed proper ancestor of the publication (`descends_trans`, `inv_descends_key`).
