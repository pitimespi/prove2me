The private lemma `pub_c`, an induction over the stable execution (whose clusters are reachable, `pstep_step`, `pexec_reach`), shows that the cluster-manager stays elected in term `t` with last published version `s.version` and last published configuration `s`'s accepted one: it does not restart, a start-join above `t` at it is excluded and one of term at most `t` fails, it cannot bootstrap (its accepted configuration has a quorum), joins keep its election won (default strategy, `quorum_mono`), no other node can publish in term `t` (`won_unique`), and the only publication of term `t` it can still accept is `s` itself (`pub_init`, from the invariant's `leader_settled`, `leader_inflight`, `leader_pubs` and `pub_unique` with `inv_descends_parent` and `inv_descends_key`). Part 1 is the private lemma `pub_q_ready`: if a node of `Q` in term `t` would reject `s`, its accepted state has term `t` and version at least `s.version`; by the reachable fact `reach_responded` (an induction over `Reachable`: only `handlePublishRequest` installs a state of term at least 1, and it sends the response) the node has acknowledged that key to some node, which published it (`response_published`), hence is the cluster-manager (`won_unique`), and whose publications of term `t` have versions at most `s.version` (`leader_pubs`); so the key is `(t, s.version)` and the acknowledgement went to the cluster-manager. Otherwise `handlePublishRequest`'s guards pass (`publish_ok`). Part 2: `handlePublishResponse`'s three guards pass (`respond_ok`), and `addVote_mem` counts the eligible sender.
