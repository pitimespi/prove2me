Case on `acceptHeader_cases`. If the header is rejected, `validate` returns that outcome (no `accept`), and `headerSpec_iff` (the if-chain characterization with `checkProofOfWork_iff` and `versionOutdated_iff`) shows `HeaderSpec` fails. If the header is accepted, `validate_accept_iff` reduces acceptance to `checkBlock ∧ contextualCheckBlock ∧ ∃ r, connectBlock = some r`, and `checkBlock_iff`, `contextualCheck_iff`, `connectBlock_iff` convert each to its predicate; the side conditions of the last two (a non-empty block, inputs in every transaction, non-negative outputs) come from `BlockSpec` through `TxSpec` and `sumValues_nonneg`. The reachability hypothesis is not needed by the argument.
