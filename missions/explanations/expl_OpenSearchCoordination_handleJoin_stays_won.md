`handleJoin_spec` gives the new votes as `addVote` of the old ones and `cs'.electionWon` as `cs.isElectionQuorum es` of the new votes, and `addVote_eligible` shows that every old voter is a new voter. The hypotheses `hinv` and `hw` make the old votes an election quorum: a quorum of the last committed configuration, a quorum of the last accepted configuration, and the strategy's constraint. `quorum_mono` carries the two quorums to the new votes and `hes` carries the constraint.
