The contested draws are contained in the union, over the pairs `i < j` of candidates, of the events `|fireTime i − fireTime j| < δ`: for a contested draw take a candidate `i0` that fires first (`Finset.exists_min_image`, the candidates being nonempty since `n ≥ 1`); since `i0` does not fire `δ` before every other candidate, some `j ≠ i0` fires less than `δ` after it, and the pair of `i0` and `j` in index order belongs to the union. The private lemma `card_pairs_lt` counts `C(n, 2)` such pairs. For one pair, the private lemma `card_pair_close_le` splits off the draw of candidate `j` (`Equiv.piSplitAt`, through the private `sum_piSplitAt`, `piSplitAt_symm_self`, `piSplitAt_symm_ne`): for each choice of the other `n − 1` draws, candidate `i`'s firing time is a fixed target, and `card_close_le` bounds the number of draws of `j` by `(2δ − 1) · 2⌈2^63/Mmin⌉`; so the event has at most `(2^64)^(n−1)` times that many draws. The union bound (`Finset.card_biUnion_le`) and the division by `(2^64)^n = (2^64)^(n−1) · 2^64` give `contestedBound n δ Mmin`.
