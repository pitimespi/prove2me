`pub_rooted` gives a descent from a term-0 key `(0, v₀)` to the publication's key. The proof strengthens the goal to every publication whose key is the end of such a descent and inducts on the descent. In the reflexive case the key has term 0, which contradicts `pub_wf` (publications have term at least 1). In the step case `pub_unique` identifies the publication with the one witnessing the last link, so its ghost parent is the previous key. If the parent's term is below the publication's term, the publication itself is first in its term. Otherwise `pub_parent` gives a sent publication with the parent's key and the same term, the induction hypothesis gives a first publication below it, and the last link extends the descent.
