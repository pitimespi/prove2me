`heightScript` is a single byte (`0` or `0x50 + n`) for `n ≤ 16` and a push of `serialize n` otherwise. A push of a non-empty element starts with a byte in `1..0x4e` and has at least two bytes (`pushBytes_shape`), so it is neither prefixed by nor a prefix of a single byte `0` or `≥ 0x51`; two single bytes are equal iff the heights are; two pushes reduce to `pushBytes_prefix` and the injectivity of `serialize` from `serialize_roundtrip`. The length bound `serialize n ≤ 5` for `n < 2^32` comes from `natBytes`.
