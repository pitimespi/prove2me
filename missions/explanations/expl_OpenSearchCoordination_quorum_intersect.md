Unfold `hasQuorum` in both hypotheses: each says that the members of `c` occurring in the vote list, counted with `List.filter`, are more than half of `c`. The private counting lemma `filter_length_add_le` (induction on the list, a case split on the two predicates at the head, `omega`) bounds the sum of the two counts by the length of `c.nodeIds` plus the number of members occurring in both lists, so that number is positive (`omega`); `List.exists_mem_of_length_pos` gives an element of the doubly filtered list, and `List.mem_filter` with `List.contains_iff_mem` turns its membership into the three conclusions.
