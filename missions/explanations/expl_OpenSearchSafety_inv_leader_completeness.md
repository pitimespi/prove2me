`inv_reign_first` gives the first publication `F` of the target publication's term, whose ghost parent is of an older term and which the target descends from. `pub_election` gives `F`'s election record over its parent; `inv_elc` puts the committed key below that parent (its term is below `F`'s term). The parent link to `F` and `descends_trans` with the descent from `F` to the target conclude.
